# Credit cycles and history: the rhymes layer

Load this for cycle-placement questions ("where are we in the cycle"), for teaching depth, and whenever an assessment would benefit from asking "what is the historical twin of this structure". Nothing here changes the twelve steps; it changes the judgment applied at step 12 and in market-pulse monitoring. History does not repeat in DeFi, but it rhymes at 10x speed.

## Minsky's classification (the actionable core)

Hyman Minsky sorted every financed position into three units, and the sort maps directly onto this skill's yield decomposition. Hedge units: cash flows cover interest and principal (a T-bill fund, a fully organic lending book). Speculative units: cash flows cover interest but principal must be refinanced (a basis book that works while funding is positive; a private credit fund that rolls maturities). Ponzi units: cash flows cover neither, so the position depends on new inflows or price appreciation (yield paid from incentives, recursion, or new deposits). Classify every product during step 3 of the assessment: source axis answers who pays, Minsky answers whether the payer can keep paying without new money. Minsky's instability hypothesis is the cycle engine: stability breeds leverage, and the system drifts hedge → speculative → Ponzi until a small shock forces simultaneous deleveraging. The drift IS observable onchain: rising loop leverage, rising incentive share, rising accrual share.

## The cycle template (Kindleberger and Dalio, condensed to observables)

The classic sequence: displacement (a genuinely new thing: a primitive, a rate regime, a legal unlock) → boom (credit expands to finance it) → euphoria (purchases financed by leverage at rising prices) → crisis (a marginal player fails, everyone rechecks collateral) → revulsion (the category trades below fair value: the reputational-contagion hunting ground). Dalio's bubble checklist, translated into DeFi-observable questions: are prices (or TVLs) high relative to the cash flows the products actually produce; are new, unsophisticated buyers arriving (front-end integrations, retail apps); is sentiment broadly and uncritically bullish; are positions financed by leverage (loop ratios, borrow-against-yield); are participants extrapolating ("this yield is the new normal"); and is a "new era" story doing the work numbers should do. The more of these answer yes, the later the cycle reads; treat a majority of yeses as a strong late-cycle prior (calibrated judgment, not a measured threshold). The deleveraging that follows is uglier onchain than offchain because there is no lender of last resort and exits run at light speed: which is why exit engineering (step 7) is the cycle hedge.

## The rhyme table: historical episode, DeFi twin, the tell

- Wildcat banknotes (US, 1830s-1860s): private par-promises backed by variable collateral, discounted by distance from the issuer. Twin: stablecoins and yieldcoins. Tell: par is an equilibrium; discounts appear the moment redemption is doubted.
- Trust companies, Panic of 1907: levered deposit-takers outside the clearinghouse safety net broke first and dragged members after. Twin: DeFi as shadow banking outside the central bank perimeter. Tell: the run starts in the least-backstopped venue.
- Broker call loans, 1929: cheap short-term credit financing levered longs on the asset being bought. Twin: looping. Tell: the financing rate and the asset price share a reflexive loop; both unwind together.
- LTCM, 1998: a levered convergence book whose trades were individually sound and collectively one crowded position. Twin: basis books and levered PT loops. Tell: correlated unwinds turn small spreads into solvency events; cluster sizing exists because of this.
- Eurodollars, 1960s-1970s: dollar intermediation moved offshore to escape domestic constraints, grew past the official sector's reach, eventually got backstopped (swap lines). Twin: the offshore crypto-dollar complex. Tell: controls relocate money rather than shrink it, and the backstop arrives after the crisis, not before.
- ABCP and repo runs, 2007-2008: short-funded vehicles holding long assets died when rollover stopped; money market funds broke the buck. Twin: instant-redemption products over slow underlyings; depegs. Tell: the first mover is rewarded, so the run is rational; gates and sleeves decide survival.
- China's wealth management products, 2010s: pooled, yield-promised, maturity-mismatched products sold through trusted distribution with implicit guarantees, until defaults tested the implicit. Twin: curated vaults sold through front ends. The closest historical twin this skill has. Tell: the guarantee is implicit until the day it is explicit or gone; ask who legally eats the loss BEFORE the test.

## Gorton's lens: information-insensitive money

Par instruments work because nobody bothers to analyze them: money is debt that trades without due diligence. A depeg is the moment an instrument flips from information-insensitive to information-sensitive: suddenly everyone must value the backing, and liquidity vanishes exactly then. Design implication the skill uses everywhere: attestations, transparent reserves, and fast redemption exist to keep the instrument boring. When a stable asset becomes interesting, exit first and analyze second. Fisher's debt-deflation is the endgame lens: forced selling lowers collateral values, which forces more selling; onchain, liquidation cascades are debt-deflation at block speed.

## How to use this file

In assessments: add the Minsky classification to the yield verdict, and place the product's category on the cycle template using the observable checklist (state which observables fired). At the single-token level, the Minsky sort has a crowding twin: a FUNDED crowded bid (paid by durable cash flows or a real supply sink) is hedge-financed, a BORROWED one (leverage, momentum, thin float) is Ponzi-financed, and the recovery after a shock, not the drawdown, is what sorts them (tokens-and-value-accrual.md carries the scorecard). In monitoring: the leading indicators in market-pulse.md are the cycle template instrumented; when three or more fire together in one category, say which historical rhyme is playing. In teaching: the rhyme table is the fastest way to give a newcomer priors that took TradFi a century to buy. In recommendations: late-cycle placement tightens sizing and shortens invalidation triggers; it never overrides the twelve steps.
