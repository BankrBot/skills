# Trade anatomy: orders, trade types, and how "neutral" books die

Load this for questions about trade execution (order types, TWAP, slippage), strategy products ("delta neutral", basis, carry, OTC deals, arbitrage), or any pitch where a fund-like product explains its "strategies." The organizing question for every strategy in this file: WHAT IS THIS BOOK SHORT? Every neutral-sounding product is short something: basis carry is short funding, volatility harvesting is short the tail, a reserve stack with an OTC bucket is short its counterparties. The name advertises the hedged leg; the fee is earned on the unhedged one (framing credit: d2 Finance, Aug 2026, a vault operator talking its own book while being right).

## 1. Order types (the execution vocabulary, identical to TradFi)

These are TradFi order types running on new rails; the definitions carry over exactly. MARKET order: execute now against the book, paying the spread and any slippage: priority is execution, not price. LIMIT order: execute at your price or better; it rests on the book until filled, canceled, or expired. Modifiers that matter for analysis: POST-ONLY (cancel rather than take: guarantees maker fees: the market maker's default), REDUCE-ONLY (can only shrink a position: the risk-management flag), TIME IN FORCE (GOOD TIL TIME auto-expires; IMMEDIATE OR CANCEL fills what it can instantly and never rests). STOP-LOSS and TAKE-PROFIT orders trigger at a mark price and then execute as market or limit: note they trigger on the venue's MARK, so oracle class (concepts 13) decides when your stop fires. TWAP (time-weighted average price) EXECUTION slices a large order into timed pieces (one venue's implementation, dated Aug 2026: a market order every 30 seconds across the chosen duration; advanced versions randomize slice timing and offer passive/neutral/aggressive styles) to reduce market impact: do not confuse a TWAP order (an execution algorithm) with a TWAP oracle (an averaged price feed); the skill uses both terms. CHASE LIMIT orders re-peg toward the touch to hold queue position. ATOMIC or basket orders execute several markets all-or-nothing (rebalances and hedges without legging risk). Venue microstructure worth knowing exists: price-time priority matching, fat-finger price bands (for example, limit prices within roughly 5% of mark), and order-margin checks that auto-cancel orders which would break an account's health. One structural novelty on the newest venues: cryptographically verified matching (Lighter generates SNARK proofs that its order book matched with price-time priority: the first exchanges where "the matcher cheated" is mathematically checkable rather than trusted): TVL prints Aug 30 2026: Lighter bridge $591.5M plus a $46.3M perp deployment on Robinhood Chain. RFQ and RFS execution are in concepts 16.

TradFi anchor: this is the NYSE/CME order vocabulary unchanged; TWAP/VWAP execution is the institutional algo desk; post-only is the maker rebate game; atomic baskets are program trading.

## 2. The trade roster (each with who pays, the short leg, and a print)

The side-by-side map first, because every onchain trade is a TradFi trade on new rails, and the column that matters is what CHANGES:

| Trade | TradFi form | DeFi form | What changes on the new rails |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash-and-carry / basis | Long spot or bonds, short the dated future, roll at expiry | Long spot, short the perp (synthetic dollars) | No expiry or roll; funding floats hourly with no circuit breaker; the venue is your counterparty |
| Carry trade | Borrow the cheap currency, buy the high yielder | Borrow stables against collateral, deploy to higher yield | The margin call is an atomic liquidation by bounty, and rates float per block |
| Convertible / restricted-stock arb | Buy the discounted PIPE or convert, short the stock | Buy locked tokens at an OTC discount, short the perp | The seller often controls the float: the counterparty can squeeze your hedge on purpose |
| Curve trade | Steepeners and flatteners in bonds and futures | PT maturities across venues | Thin maturities; marks come from a chosen oracle, not a deep tape |
| Option selling / structured yield | Covered-call funds and put-write indices priced off implied volatility | Covered-call vaults; every concentrated LP range (options file) | Premium is paid by realized flow, not implied volatility; no dynamic hedge |
| Cross-listing arb | ADR vs local share; ETF price vs NAV | CEX/DEX gaps; canonical vs bridged versions | Bridge and settlement latency: inventory is in flight when the price moves |
| Market making | Specialist and HFT quoting for spread and rebates | AMM LP, prop AMMs, CLOB quoting | Passive LPs sell volatility without vega compensation; JIT and LVR eat headline APR |
| Merger / event arb | M&A spreads with legal completion mechanics | Largely absent (prediction markets are out of this skill's scope) | Say the twin does not exist rather than inventing one |

FUNDING-RATE ARBITRAGE (long perp on one venue, short on another, or long/short across correlated names): paid by directional traders through funding; short the CONVERGENCE assumption and venue risk on both legs. BASIS TRADE (long spot, short perp or future): paid by longs' funding when positive; short FUNDING itself: the carry inverts exactly in stress (perps file). PT RATE ARBITRAGE (cross-venue or cross-maturity principal-token pricing): short liquidity at maturity boundaries. CROSS-VENUE ARB: short bridge and settlement latency (the inventory is in flight when the price moves). LP AND MARKET MAKING: short volatility (options file): the premium is fees, the tail is LVR and inventory. Every roster entry obeys concepts 4: name who pays, then ask why they keep paying.

## 3. The centerpiece: the discounted locked-token OTC trade (three sizes of the same blowup, all 2026, verified)

The trade: an OTC (over-the-counter) desk or token team sells LOCKED or vesting tokens at 20% to 60% below market; the buyer shorts the same token on perps until unlock, calling the package delta neutral and booking the discount as carry. Decompose the discount exactly like a yield, because it is one: it pays you for CLIFF risk (no exit until unlock), HEDGE-CARRY risk (the short pays funding, which goes deeply negative precisely when the token is squeezed: and when the seller controls the float, the squeeze is a choice they can make against you), ADVERSE SELECTION (whoever sells at 60% off knows the unlock schedule, the market-maker arrangements, and the float), and MARK risk (a locked position has no honest price until it unlocks). TradFi anchors: PIPE (private investment in public equity) discounts and Rule 144 restricted stock, where discounts price resale restrictions; convertible desks shorting against restricted positions; the squeeze-against-hedgers dynamic retail knows from GameStop.

The sell side, LAB (zachxbt investigation, May to July 2026): a token with reported >95% insider supply control ran to a $6B+ FDV while the team offered private menus (loans at 5 to 7.5% per month; OTC at 60% off with cliffs; "guaranteed discount" tranches), unilaterally moved a public sale's cliff from 3 to 9 months, and coordinated market makers; it later fell 85% in a day. The information map to memorize: the team knows the unlocks, the MM knows the positioning, OTC buyers know their cliffs; retail knows only the price.

The small buyer, PiggyBank (self-disclosed, June 6 2026): a Solana yield protocol bought ~$100K of locked LAB at a discount via a "Tier-1 OTC desk," hedged with perp shorts, then closed the hedge when deeply negative funding made it "economically irrational," leaving a naked locked long. Honest mechanics followed: the position was excluded from NAV until unlock and vault NAVs were written down 9 to 15%. Two findings travel: STRATEGY DRIFT (their docs described spot basis at under 1x leverage; locked OTC inventory is not spot: compare every position's shape to the documented mandate) and the docs' phrase "risk free arbitrage," which is always a finding. Protocol TVL was $1.6M by Aug 30 2026 (DefiLlama).

The protocol scale, Neutrl (reported Aug 2026): a "market-neutral synthetic dollar unlocking yield in OTC and altcoin markets" (their docs; example allocation 20% liquid reserves, 20% hedged OTC positions, 60% market-neutral strategies; the load-bearing claim: locked tokens "hedged to eliminate price risk": hedging converts price risk into carry and counterparty risk, it does not eliminate it). Sequence per multi-outlet reporting and the d2 Finance timeline: Terms of Use amended Aug 11 to disclaim any obligation to honor redemptions and reserve a right to freeze; minting and redemptions paused Aug 13/14 citing "circumstances affecting protocol reserves"; a suspected team address withdrew ~$3.5M from the Curve pool 14 minutes before the announcement; sNUSD traded near $0.56; an Aug 29 disclosure reported ~$27M liquid against a $137M June dashboard (which had shown a $12.5M OTC aggregate bucket). Root cause remained UNCONFIRMED by the team as of Aug 30 2026; the community hypothesis is the locked-OTC hedge bleeding under manipulated negative funding. Not listed on DefiLlama as of the same date: low observability is itself a scored finding.

The pre-funded contrast: Ethena, the largest delta-neutral book ($4.08B USDe TVL, DefiLlama Aug 30 2026), maintains a RESERVE FUND designed to absorb periods of negative funding so stress hits a buffer instead of the backing: pre-funding the short leg is what serious operators do. The live caveat that proves the skill's method: per Ethena's own docs (Aug 2026), the ongoing revenue allocation to that fund was 0% (100% to incentives), with the fund retaining a prior balance: a governance-set parameter to CHECK on the transparency dashboard, not assume. The TradFi anchor for the whole section: LTCM, the most market-neutral book of its era, ended in 1998 with a roughly $3.6B recapitalization by a private consortium brokered by the New York Fed: no public money, but a lesson that "neutral" books die through their financing and their crowd, not their models' averages.

## 4. The Terms-of-Service tell (new monitoring surface)

Neutrl's exit clause was rewritten three days before the gate. Encode it generally: the legal terms are a parameter set with an owner, and a quiet amendment to redemption obligations, freeze rights, or liability language is the legal-layer equivalent of an untimelocked admin key being exercised. Monitoring should DIFF THE TERMS (archive them, compare on a cadence, alert on redemption/freeze language changes) the way it watches timelocks, and the service-provider map (concepts 14) gains a line: who can amend the terms, with what notice. NAV mechanics under illiquidity complete the picture: the honest sequence is side-pocket style (exclude the illiquid position from NAV, gate that sleeve, disclose: the 2008 hedge fund playbook, and what PiggyBank did); the dishonest one is an unnamed hole, an amended ToS, and insiders exiting the pool first.

## 5. The crowding overlay (when a whole market is in your trade)

Before sizing any strategy, ask whether the trade is CROWDED and whether the crowd is FUNDED or BORROWED (framework: Delphi Digital's "The Crowded Book," Aug 2026; note the authors disclosed possibly holding the names they scored best). Funded means the bid is paid for by something durable: real cash flows, organic demand, a supply sink that retires float. Borrowed means leverage, momentum, and thin float: strength on loan from the tape. Their five 0-to-5 tests work as a scorecard on any crowded name: revenue backing, float depth, holder breadth, leverage/reflexivity (can the structure run in reverse), and recovery quality after a shock (the only outcome-based test: in their June 2026 case study, the fee-funded name recovered 135% of its drop, the scarcity name 85%, the thin-float name 52% and stayed down). Tokens file carries the accrual side; credit-cycles carries the Minsky mapping (borrowed crowding is the Ponzi-financed bid). For this file the use is direct: a short leg against a BORROWED bid with a controlling seller is not a hedge, it is being the counterparty of the squeeze; and "everyone does this trade" means the exit door is priced for one.

## 6. The checklist for any strategy product (run with the main checklist)

What is the book short, and is that leg pre-funded (reserve fund sized how, contributing at what current rate)? Does each live position's SHAPE match the documented strategy taxonomy (locked is not spot; OTC inventory is not basis)? How are illiquid positions marked and are they in or out of NAV? Who can amend the Terms, and when did they last change? What does the discount or spread decompose into, slice by slice? Is the trade crowded, and funded or borrowed? And the constant: who knows what you do not (unlocks, positioning, cliffs)?
