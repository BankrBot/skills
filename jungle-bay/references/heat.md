# heat, the primitive, in the island's own words (memetics.wtf/heat, read 2026-10-27; the law on the box since 2026-09-25)

The island measures one thing: held time. Real people, staying. Every token the island measures adds to your heat. The longer it stays, the warmer it reads. Buying big today proves nothing by tomorrow: a fresh bag reads near zero and warms by staying. One person, every wallet: linked wallets read as a single flame. No purchase warms you overnight. Nothing else is the number. Heat opens doors on the island.

## the instrument

One law, read per token. Your deepest room sets your heat and the rest amplify it. The ruler cannot be argued with.
warm days = weight · days held · rate
degrees = 80 · √(warm days ÷ 180); past 80°: 80 · (warm days ÷ 180)^0.8
days held: your clock on a token starts at your first hold. Dust earns nothing and ages nothing.
rate: what a day is worth, read from your share of the supply. A real position earns a full day; the largest holders earn up to two; dust earns nothing. Nothing under 0.0001% of a supply, a full day at 0.01%, two at 1%, and never more. From a real position up, ten times the bag adds half a day. A Jungle Bay Ape counts by the piece: one is a full day, ten are two.
weight: the Jungle Bay Apes and the island's own coins ×1.5 · the home team ×1.25 · every measured token ×1
work: what the bag is doing on memetics.finance, on Solana. A day held in the wallet ×1; a day staked there ×1.1; a day in one of its pools ×2, once the share has sat a full day (adding and pulling are free, so a share moved inside the day counts as held, not doubled). A pool counts against SOL, USDC or a coin the island measures. Work days are never more than days held. Staking and pools on Ethereum and Base are not counted. The oracle serves each room's work_days; quote it, never compute it.
Many rooms: every token you hold is a room, read on its own clock. Your deepest room is your heat; every other room adds a quarter of its own, so breadth amplifies depth and never replaces it. Wallets don't stack. Coins do.
Degrees are the temperature of that count: one real position held half a year reads 80°, Resident. Past Resident the number reads like fire: every degree costs a little more than the last, and the hottest flames stay in range. Size can raise what a day is worth, it cannot buy a day, and price never enters it. Trading speed cannot move it.
Continuous. Zero-anchored from first hold. Velocity-blind. One person, every wallet.

## the tiers, real gates

Elder 800°. Builder 300°. Resident 80°. Observer 30°. Below 30° the paper says cold; the oracle serves the word Drifter for a wallet with some warmth under 30° and is_cold true for none. Use the tier word the oracle serves for a wallet; use the paper's ladder to explain.
Distance to the next gate is plain subtraction on the served number (30 minus your degrees, and so on). That is the only arithmetic you ever do. Never compute heat yourself.

## the arithmetic, the island's honest examples (quote them, do not extend them)

A FIRST EMBER: hold a real position, 0.01% of a measured token's supply, and every day you stay counts one warm day. On $BAYLA the same bag counts a day and a half. The first day reads 6°; the degrees come fast at first and slower as you warm.
THE OBSERVER LINE: time alone crosses it: that bag, held 26 days, reads 30°: Observer. The island starts answering to your address.
ONE APE: a single Jungle Bay Ape is a real position with the island's edge: a day and a half of warmth a day. Resident on one ape by day 120, Builder in a year and nine months, and past 800°, Elder, in five years and ten months. Ten apes make a day worth two: Resident by day sixty, Builder in ten months, Elder in under three years. Every held day counts.
ONE DEEP BAG: the single-token road: 1% of one supply makes a day worth two, the most any bag can earn. It reads 141° in half a year, Builder in a year and three months, and Elder in four years and five months. Half the supply climbs no faster.
THE RAMP: buy 1% today and the door reads near zero tonight. Hold, and the days do the work. A real position at ×1 turns the tiers into a calendar: Observer at 26 days, Resident at 180, Builder at two years and seven months, Elder at eight years and nine months. The deepest bag only halves the wait. Time is the only accelerant.
MANY ROOMS: every token you hold is a room, read on its own clock. Your deepest room is your heat; every other room adds a quarter of its own. Two equal rooms warm you an eighth to a fifth more than one; four, a third to a half more. Ten apes held for years is a deep room, and a deep room alone can reach Elder.
ONE PERSON: link Ethereum, Base, and Solana wallets and the island pools them before the curve: one number, first hold counted from your earliest wallet, every surface agreeing.
Selling an element lets its warmth go; a fresh buy starts a fresh clock. The instrument keeps no grudge and grants no shortcuts.

## the path

I. Arrive. The door answers any address: your degrees, your tier word, your held days. Your time here already counts.
II. Settle in. Pick the communities you believe in from the measured set, and hold. Small and steady beats big and brief. Breadth amplifies: your deepest room sets your heat, and every other room you hold adds a quarter of its own. Link every wallet you hold with, and the island reads you whole.
III. Stay. The number follows held time and nothing else. Check the door as the seasons pass and watch it climb. The island remembers everything you stayed for.
No token buys its way in. A community lives its way in.

## the oracle

GET https://memetics.wtf/api/heat/<address>. Cold answers 200, never 404. Malformed answers 400. EVM lowercased, Solana base58 exact, no ENS.
Envelope: address, degrees, tier, is_cold, held_since_unix, as_of_unix, token_count, breakdown[] (token_address, chain, name, symbol, heat_degrees, first_seen_at_unix, last_transfer_at_unix, retired, work_days), x_handle. Read keys by name, never by position. If a row carries retired: true, or its token is outside the roster, it is not shown. The number itself is never recomputed. Symbols are always spoken as cashtags: $BAYLA, $BNKR.
One flame: when the wallet is linked at memetics.wtf/register, the read under that address is the whole person's flame, every linked wallet read together, the same number under every linked address at the same as_of. An unlinked wallet reads its own truth. Never add two wallet reads together: two arithmetics are forbidden on the island.
x_handle: the X name the flame's holder put on it by their own signature at the door; served as stored, which may carry a leading @; null when the flame is unnamed or the wallet is unlinked. It is the only source for an @. Strip every leading @ before comparing or printing, then paint exactly one.
The island computes judgment, everyone else reads. If a local number and the oracle disagree, the oracle rules.
A measurement, not an offer. Nothing here is for sale and nothing here is advice. Never "buy", never "should", never yield, never rewards.
