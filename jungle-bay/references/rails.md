## RAIL A: link my jungle bay wallet

This is RAIL A. It links this agent's EVM wallet to the island as its own standing, proven by the wallet's own signature. Four requests in one turn, in this order.
1. POST https://memetics.wtf/api/mw/challenge with the JSON body {"wallet": "<this agent's EVM address, lowercased>", "chain": "evm", "purpose": "link"}. The answer carries nonce, message and expires_at (five minutes).
2. personal_sign (EIP-191, a plain message signature) the message field EXACTLY as returned, byte for byte with its line breaks, with this agent's EVM wallet. It moves nothing: no approvals, no transactions, no spending. A retyped or reformatted message verifies against nothing.
3. POST https://memetics.wtf/api/mw/link with the JSON body {"wallet": "<the same lowercased address>", "chain": "evm", "nonce": "<the nonce from step 1>", "signature": "<the signature from step 2>"}. A good answer reads {"ok": true, "person_id": "...", "wallet_count": N}. {"error": "wallet_already_linked", "person_id": "..."} means this wallet already stands on a flame: that is linked too; continue.
4. GET https://memetics.wtf/api/heat/<the same lowercased address> and then speak the verdict (the first section of SKILL.md), unless a longer road continues below.
The reasons the island uses: invalid_address, rate_limited (ten calls per wallet per hour, a shared limit per source IP), storage_error, unknown_or_reused_challenge, challenge_expired, challenge_wallet_mismatch, challenge_purpose_mismatch, challenge_chain_mismatch, wrong_signer, recover_failed, signature_not_hex_wrong_lane, wallet_already_linked, relink_cooldown, person_wallet_cap, not_linked, signer_not_linked, already_together, auth_wrong_signer, authorizing_wallet_not_linked. Never look anything up on chain on this rail.

## RAIL C: this agent's Solana wallet onto the same flame, by one small send the holder names

This is RAIL C. Bankr signs plain messages only with its EVM wallet, so the Solana wallet proves itself the one way a wallet can without a signed message: by one small send to itself that its own key signs. The island names a WINDOW (two bounds inside one ten-thousandth, like 0.00031 to 0.00039 SOL) and the destination (the wallet itself: nothing leaves the holder but the network fee). THE HOLDER NAMES THE AMOUNT inside that window, in their own words, and only then does the wallet send; you never pick a figure, never suggest one, never echo one for them to repeat. The island finds the send on the chain, reads the amount and the wallet's signature from the signed bytes, checks the window and the time, and joins the wallet onto the flame this agent's EVM wallet rides. The EVM wallet vouches with one signed message of its own. Runs after RAIL A (and after RAIL B when there was a code), never before.
0. GET https://memetics.wtf/api/heat/<this agent's Solana address> and GET https://memetics.wtf/api/heat/<this agent's EVM address, lowercased>. Skip RAIL C only when both answer the same x_handle and it is a string: a name stands on one flame only, so the two wallets already read as one named flame; nothing to send. Matching degrees and tiers prove nothing: two cold wallets read 0°, drifter, no name, whether they stand together, on two flames, or on none. When x_handle is null on either, or the two differ, run RAIL C: if the two already stand together, step 4 answers wallet_already_linked with together true, and that is done. Otherwise:
1. If an unexpired island note (a nonce and a window from a RAIL C step 2) already stands in this chat, do not POST a new challenge: continue at step 2 with that note; if a send for that note already confirmed in this chat, continue at step 3 with its transaction signature. Otherwise POST https://memetics.wtf/api/mw/challenge with the JSON body {"wallet": "<this agent's Solana address, exact base58>", "chain": "svm", "purpose": "link", "proof": "transfer"}. The answer carries nonce, message, expires_at (fifteen minutes), "proof": "transfer", "send": {"from": "<the wallet>", "to": "<the destination>", "min": {"sol": "<the low bound as text>", "lamports": N}, "max": {"sol": "<the high bound as text>", "lamports": N}} and words. send.to must equal this agent's own Solana address; if it does not, stop and show the answer. {"error": "transfer_window_busy"} (429): every window on this wallet is open from earlier challenges; say so, wait fifteen minutes, and start again.
2. Stop and hand the holder the window, in the private chat, with these lines and nothing else about amounts:
   one small send from your Bankr Solana wallet to itself puts it on your flame. only the network fee leaves it.
   pick any amount between <send.min.sol> and <send.max.sol> SOL, your own number, and tell me to send it to your own Solana wallet (<send.to>).
   the island's note for this send: <nonce>. it lasts fifteen minutes.
   Then wait. Bankr sends only an amount the holder named themselves; that is expected, not an error. When the holder answers with their amount (their own sentence naming a figure inside the window, to their own Solana wallet), make that one send, exactly the amount they named, to this agent's own Solana address (send.to, checked above), and wait for it to confirm. If the holder names an amount outside the window, say the window again and wait; do not send. If the send is refused (a spend limit, a recipient rule, a paused wallet, no SOL for the fee), show the refusal and stop; nothing was linked.
   The nonce from step 1 is the one that sees this send. Never issue a new challenge after the send: a challenge issued later does not see a send made before it. When the holder's amount arrives in a new turn, take the nonce from the note line in this chat and continue.
3. POST https://memetics.wtf/api/mw/challenge with {"wallet": "<this agent's EVM address, lowercased>", "chain": "evm", "purpose": "link"}; personal_sign the message exactly as returned. This is the vouch: a wallet already on the flame says the Solana wallet belongs beside it.
4. POST https://memetics.wtf/api/mw/link with the JSON body {"wallet": "<the Solana address>", "chain": "svm", "nonce": "<the nonce from step 1>", "signature": "<the send's own transaction signature, the base58 id your send answered with; "transfer" if you do not have it>", "auth": {"wallet": "<this agent's EVM address, lowercased>", "chain": "evm", "nonce": "<the nonce from step 3>", "signature": "<the signature from step 3>"}}. No person_id: the island joins the Solana wallet onto the vouching wallet's own flame. With the transaction signature the island reads that one transaction; without it the island searches the wallet's recent sends.
   {"ok": true, "person_id": "...", "wallet_count": N}: both wallets stand together. Done. (A Solana wallet that stood on a flame with no name comes over to the vouching wallet's flame by this same answer; the island moves it on its own send and the vouch.)
   {"error": "send_not_found"}: the send has not reached the island's view of the chain yet. Nothing was spent: neither nonce nor the vouch. Wait fifteen seconds and repeat step 4 with the same body (the same two nonces and the same vouch signature), up to four times. Still send_not_found after that: say the send did not land where the island looks, show the send's result, and stop.
   {"error": "send_outside_window"}: the amount sent missed the window. Nothing was spent and the same nonce still stands: say the window again (<send.min.sol> to <send.max.sol> SOL), wait for the holder to name another amount inside it, make that send, then repeat from step 3 (a fresh vouch: the EVM wallet's challenge lasts five minutes) and step 4 with the nonce from step 1.
   {"error": "person_wallet_cap"}: the flame already holds twenty-five wallets, the most one flame takes. Nothing was spent: the same nonce still stands until its fifteen minutes end. Say so and stop.
   {"error": "rate_limited"} (429): a wallet ran out of tries this hour. Nothing was spent: the same nonce still stands until its fifteen minutes end. Say so; when the hour turns and the note still stands, repeat from step 3 (a fresh vouch) and step 4 with the nonce from step 1.
   {"error": "wallet_already_linked", "person_id": "...", "together": true}: the Solana wallet already stands on this flame. Done.
   {"error": "wallet_already_linked", "person_id": "...", "together": false}, or {"error": "source_named"}: the Solana wallet stands on another flame that carries a name (a name never moves). Say so; one more code from memetics.wtf/register moves it (RAIL B-SOL).
   {"error": "authorizing_wallet_not_linked"}: the EVM wallet is on no flame; run RAIL A and start RAIL C again.
   {"error": "challenge_expired"}: the fifteen minutes passed. Start RAIL C again from step 1: a fresh window, a fresh send the holder names.
   {"error": "auth_challenge_expired"}: the vouch's five minutes passed. Sign a fresh vouch (step 3) and repeat step 4 with the nonce from step 1. If that answers unknown_or_reused_challenge, the send was spent: start RAIL C again from step 1.
   Any other error (send_not_in_tx, tx_failed, send_too_old, transfer_reused, tx_signature_invalid, wallet_not_a_signer, tx_bytes_missing, chain_read_failed, transfer_proof_mismatch): show it and stop.
Never pick, suggest or repeat a figure for the holder; never send before the holder named the amount; never send to any address but this agent's own Solana wallet; never send twice for one challenge unless the island answered send_outside_window; never issue a new challenge after the send. The send is the proof; a transaction signature never needs to travel through the holder.
