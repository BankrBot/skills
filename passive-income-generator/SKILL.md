# Passive Income Generator Skill

## Overview
This skill guides the discovery, execution, and monitoring of sustainable on-chain passive income workflows across EVM chains (Base, Arbitrum, Mainnet, Polygon) and Solana.

## Strategy Categories

### 1. Liquid Staking & Yield-Bearing Collateral
- Convert idle assets (ETH, SOL) into liquid staking tokens (LSTs) such as wstETH, cbETH, or JitoSOL to capture native consensus yields with zero active management.
- Hold yield-bearing stablecoins (e.g., sDAI, sUSDe, USDM) for baseline dollar-denominated cash flow.

### 2. Money Markets & Lending
- Supply high-quality collateral to battle-tested lending protocols (e.g., Aave v3, Compound v3) to earn variable deposit APY plus protocol incentives.
- Monitor utilization rates and borrow demands to optimize allocation across venues.

### 3. Liquidity Provision & Yield Farming
- Deposit into concentrated or classic AMM liquidity pools (e.g., Aerodrome Slipstream on Base, Uniswap v3) for high trading-fee capture and emission rewards.
- Target stable-pair pools (e.g., USDC-USDbC, LST-ETH) to minimize impermanent loss risk.

### 4. Automated Compounding & Rebalancing
- Establish scheduled workflows to harvest earned yield tokens, convert proceeds to base assets, and compound principal balances.

## Risk Assessment Framework
Before recommending or executing any strategy, evaluate:
- Smart contract risk and audit history of the venue.
- Liquidity depth and exit slippage.
- Impermanent loss profile for volatile LP pairs.
- Peg stability for synthetic and yield-bearing assets.
- Health factor and liquidation thresholds for collateralized positions.

## Operational Workflow
1. Assess current wallet balances and asset distribution.
2. Filter suitable strategies based on risk tolerance (low risk: LSTs/lending; moderate risk: stable LPs; higher risk: volatile LP pairs).
3. Present yield comparisons, net APY, fee structure, and risk factors.
4. Execute transactions cleanly upon user confirmation.
5. Track active positions, accrued yields, and notify when rebalancing or harvesting is optimal.
