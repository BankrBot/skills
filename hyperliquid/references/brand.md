# Brand assets

`logo.svg` is the official Hyperliquid mark, taken unmodified from the
[Hyperliquid brand kit](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/brand-kit)
("Hyperliquid SVG format" archive, `Hyperliquid_Blob_Green.svg`): the 200×200
blob in the brand mint `#97FCE4`, on a transparent background.

The same kit ships:

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `Hyperliquid_Blob_Green.svg` | the mark on a dark surface (this file) |
| `Hyperliquid_Blob_Dark.svg` | the mark in `#011916` on a light surface |
| `Hyperliquid_Blob_Light.svg` | the mark in white on a dark surface |
| `Hyperliquid_Dark.svg` / `Hyperliquid_Light.svg` | the full wordmark |

Use the mark to refer to Hyperliquid itself, not to imply endorsement of this
skill. `hyperliquid` is the CLI from
[hypurr-inc/hyperliquid-cli](https://github.com/hypurr-inc/hyperliquid-cli) (MIT);
the brand assets remain Hyperliquid's.
